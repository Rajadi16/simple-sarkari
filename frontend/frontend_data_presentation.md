# Frontend Data Presentation Requirements

This document outlines the JSON schema details and the information available from the backend that needs to be presented on the frontend of the website. The data is based on the `CanonicalCircular` and `Translation` models.

## 1. Circular Core Identity
The primary identity of the government circular.
- `id` (String): Unique identifier for the circular.
- `identity.title_original` (String): The original title of the document.
- `identity.document_number` (String, Optional): The official document number.
- `identity.reference_number` (String, Optional): The reference number.
- `identity.gazette_number` (String, Optional): The official gazette number.
- `identity.subject_original` (String, Optional): The original subject of the document.

## 2. Classification & Categorization
How the circular is categorized within the government structure.
- `classification.government_level` (String): "central" or "state".
- `classification.state` (String, Optional): The state if it's a state-level document.
- `classification.department` (String, Optional): The issuing department.
- `classification.document_type` (String, Optional): e.g., press_release, order, circular.
- `classification.category` (String, Optional): Broad category.
- `classification.sub_category` (String, Optional): Sub-category.
- `classification.language` (String): Original language, e.g., "en-IN".

## 3. Dates & Timelines
Crucial dates for the citizen to know when this circular applies.
- `dates.published_date` (String, Optional): ISO 8601 date string.
- `dates.effective_from` (String, Optional): When the rules take effect.
- `dates.effective_until` (String, Optional): When the rules expire.
- `dates.last_updated` (String, Optional): Last update timestamp.

## 4. AI Simplification (Citizen-Facing Info)
The core value proposition of the app, generated by the AI to help citizens understand the circular.
- `simplification.simplified_title` (String, Optional): Easy to understand title.
- `simplification.summary` (String, Optional): A brief summary of the circular.
- `simplification.simplified_text` (String, Optional): Full simplified text.
- `simplification.required_action` (String, Optional): What the citizen needs to do.
- `simplification.who_is_affected` (String, Optional): Target audience for the circular.
- `simplification.important_dates` (List of Objects): Specific important dates mentioned in the text.
- `simplification.amounts` (List of Objects): Any financial amounts, fines, or grants mentioned.
- `simplification.eligibility` (List of Strings): Who is eligible (if applicable).
- `simplification.keywords` (List of Strings): Tags for searchability.
- `simplification.key_points` (List of Strings): Bullet points of the most important takeaways.
- `simplification.action_items` (List of Strings): Specific actions required.
- `simplification.deadlines` (List of Objects): Critical deadlines.
- `simplification.warnings` (List of Strings): Penalties or important warnings.
- `simplification.source_excerpts` (List of Strings): Quotes from the original text for reference.

## 5. Source Information
Where the document originally came from.
- `source.source_name` (String): Name of the source (e.g., PIB, DoPT).
- `source.source_url` (String): URL of the source portal.
- `source.official_document_url` (String, Optional): Direct link to the original document on the government site.

## 6. Original Content & Attachments
For citizens who want to read the original document.
- `content.clean_text` (String, Optional): The extracted text without boilerplate.
- `content.sections` (List of Objects): Heading, text, page_start, page_end.
- `attachments` (List of Objects): 
  - `url`: Link to the attachment.
  - `type`: File type (e.g., "pdf").
  - `title`: Title of the attachment.
  - `file_size_bytes`: Size of the file.

## 7. Translations & Audio
Available via separate API endpoints (e.g., `/api/circulars/{id}/translations/{lang}` and `/api/circulars/{id}/audio/{lang}`).
- `translated_title` (String)
- `translated_text` (String)
- `translated_summary` (String)
- `audio_url` (String - from the audio API endpoint)

---
### Presentation Guidelines for the Frontend:
1. **Hero Section**: Highlight the `simplified_title`, `summary`, and `who_is_affected`.
2. **Key Actionables**: Prominently display `required_action`, `deadlines`, and `key_points`.
3. **Details Grid**: Show `department`, `published_date`, `government_level`.
4. **Deep Dive**: Provide toggleable tabs for `simplified_text`, `original_text`, and `translations`.
5. **Downloads**: Clear buttons for `attachments` (Original PDF) and audio versions.
